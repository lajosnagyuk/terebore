Find your little rhythm.

Point at the floor or either wall to choose your first bounce. The dots follow your aim. Click to throw. On touch, drag to move the aim point above your finger, then release to throw.

When three or more marbles of the same colour touch, they turn into a soft puff. Each marble brings 10 points. Larger groups bring a little bonus.

Try bouncing before the wooden retaining rail, landing inside it, or banking off a wall. The dots suggest up to four room bounces, fading as the path becomes less certain. At the rail or pile, the guide leaves the outcome to your throw.

Let each throw land before the next. A wall-assisted match earns 15 extra points; a chain reaction earns a little more. Matching marbles brighten together, then clear. Clearing the inside triangle earns 25 extra points, even if balls remain outside the rail.

Two rare colours add a little surprise: Light softly glows and completes a colour’s group of three. It can complete several colour groups at once, but cannot combine different colours to reach three. Light ignites all connected Clay balls, but Clay alone never clears. Clay stays rare. Each match increases the chance of your next Light, until one arrives in your pocket and the chance resets.

Perfect colours have a soft inner glow and match their ordinary colour. Each Perfect adds another half to the match score. Matching a colour makes its Perfect more likely; receiving that Perfect resets its chance. The colours beneath the logo glow as their chances grow, with a soft white underline for Light. A quiet underline means the chance is back at its base.

No timer. No game over. Take your time.

Keyboard: Arrow keys to move your aim · Space to throw · Esc to close
